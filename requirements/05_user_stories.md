# User Stories

## Epic 1: Stock Discovery and Ranking

### US1.1: Set Investment Priority
As a **retail investor**, I want to **select my investment priority (ROI, growth, or dividends)**, so that **I can view stocks ranked by what matters most to me**.

### US1.2: View Ranked Stock List
As a **retail investor**, I want to **see a ranked list of top stocks based on my chosen priority**, so that **I can quickly identify the best stocks for my investment goals**.

### US1.3: Access Stock Details
As a **retail investor**, I want to **view additional details about each stock (price, sector, market cap)**, so that **I can make a more informed decision about potential investments**.

### US1.4: Change Investment Criteria
As a **retail investor**, I want to **easily change my investment priority and see updated results**, so that **I can explore different investment strategies without friction**.

---

## Epic 2: Fast and Simple User Experience

### US2.1: Quick Access to Results
As a **busy investor**, I want to **receive ranked stock recommendations within 2 minutes of setting my criteria**, so that **I can make timely investment decisions without excessive waiting**.

### US2.2: Understand the Platform Immediately
As a **first-time user**, I want to **use the platform without training or instructions**, so that **I can get value from it immediately without frustration**.

### US2.3: Minimal Interaction Required
As a **casual investor**, I want to **complete all critical actions (setting criteria, viewing results) in 3 clicks or fewer**, so that **I can focus on decision-making rather than navigating complexity**.

---

## Epic 3: Data Accuracy and Trust

### US3.1: Verify Stock Metrics
As a **cautious investor**, I want to **trust that stock metrics (ROI, growth, dividends) are accurate and current**, so that **my investment decisions are based on reliable information**.

### US3.2: Understand Metric Definitions
As a **investor with varying financial knowledge**, I want to **know exactly how each metric is calculated**, so that **I can trust and understand the platform's rankings**.

---

## Epic 4: Continuous Access and Reliability

### US4.1: Access Platform Anytime
As a **retail investor**, I want to **access the stock analysis platform during normal business hours**, so that **I can research stocks when it matters most to my trading schedule**.

### US4.2: Consistent Performance
As a **user**, I want to **have a fast, responsive experience regardless of how many other users are on the platform**, so that **I can reliably complete my analysis without delays**.

---

## Epic 5: Personalization and Preferences

### US5.1: Save Investment Preferences
As a **returning investor**, I want to **have my investment priority remembered across sessions**, so that **I don't have to re-enter my preferences every time I visit**.

### US5.2: Compare Multiple Strategies
As a **investor exploring options**, I want to **quickly switch between different investment priorities and compare results**, so that **I can understand how different strategies rank stocks differently**.

---

## Backlog: Out of Scope (Future Enhancements)
The following user stories are documented for future consideration but are **not included in the MVP**:

- As a user, I want to execute trades directly from the platform
- As a user, I want real-time stock price updates
- As a user, I want to track my personal portfolio
- As a user, I want access to advanced technical analysis charts
- As a user, I want to receive alerts when stock metrics change
- As a user, I want to compare my selections with other users' choices
