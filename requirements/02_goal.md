# Goals and Objectives

## Goals

### Goal 1: Enable Quick Stock Analysis
Users should be able to identify top stocks within minutes, not hours.

**Success Metric:** Users can retrieve and view their top 10 stocks in under 2 minutes from initial access.

### Goal 2: Simplify Stock Prioritization
Users should easily define and apply their investment priorities without confusion or complexity.

**Success Metric:** 90% of users can set their investment criteria (ROI, growth, dividends) on first attempt without assistance.

### Goal 3: Personalize Stock Rankings
Users should see stocks ranked according to their specific priorities, not generic lists.

**Success Metric:** User satisfaction score of 8+/10 when comparing personalized rankings to default stock lists.

### Goal 4: Reduce Analysis Complexity
The tool interface and data presentation should not overwhelm users with unnecessary information.

**Success Metric:** Users report that the platform is "simple to use" with 85% rating it as intuitive.

### Goal 5: Support Informed Decision-Making
Users should have confidence in stock recommendations based on clear, transparent metrics.

**Success Metric:** 80% of users report increased confidence in their stock selection decisions after using the platform.

## Success Metrics Summary
- Time to identify top stocks: ≤ 2 minutes
- First-time setup success rate: ≥ 90%
- User satisfaction (simplicity): ≥ 8.5/10
- Intuitiveness rating: ≥ 85%
- Decision confidence improvement: ≥ 80%
